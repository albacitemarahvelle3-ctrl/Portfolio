About Me

I bridge the gap between classroom instruction and hands-on technical engineering. As an educator at Julio Ledesma National High School, I design curricula for English, Electronics, Robotics, and Computer-Aided Design. I guide student teams through competitive environments, from the National Schools Press Conference to the World Robot Olympiad.

Beyond the classroom, I am deeply involved in emerging technologies. I co-founded Web3 San Carlos to foster local blockchain education, and I actively develop game systems in Roblox Studio utilizing Lua and Luau.
